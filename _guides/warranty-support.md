## Where to find the serial number

Every support request goes faster with the serial number. It's printed on the round QR tag on the heater, and it appears on the Heater Control page when you tap that heater.

## Service and repairs

{% include warn.html text="The heater has no user-serviceable parts. All service and repairs beyond basic cleaning must be done by Focal-authorized personnel." %}

If a unit is damaged or malfunctioning, stop using it, secure any loose parts, and contact Focal for a loaner or replacement rather than attempting a repair.

## Warranty

Focal heaters carry a 3-year warranty. To make a claim, contact Focal Support with the heater's serial number and a description of the issue.

## Disposal

The product contains recyclable materials. Don't put it in household waste. Contact Focal for returns and disposal.
