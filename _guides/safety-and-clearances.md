## Read this first

Read these instructions before installing or operating. Improper installation, use, or maintenance can cause serious injury or property damage. Use only as directed.

Focal heaters are rated for indoor and covered outdoor use. Install only where the unit is protected from rain, spray, and standing water.

{% include warn.html text="Failure to follow these instructions could result in fire, electric shock, injury, or death." %}

## Warnings

- Use the heater only as described in these guides. Any other use may cause fire, electric shock, or injury.
- Use only the rail and heater configurations Focal specifies. Never exceed the maximum number of heaters per rail.
- Do not open or tamper with the interior of the heater.
- Do not keep combustible materials near the heaters, including furniture, awnings, fabric, paper, and decorations.
- Do not hang anything from the heater or its mounting brackets.
- Do not operate near flammable vapors or liquids such as gasoline or solvents.

{% include warn.html text="The exterior surface can exceed 70°C (158°F) during operation. The only part anyone should touch is the pull string." %}

## Electrical safety

- The heater runs on 120VAC, 60 Hz. Connect only to a properly installed, GFCI-protected, dedicated outlet.
- Outdoors, use a weather-protected outlet with an in-use weatherproof cover.
- Do not use the heater if the power cord or plug is damaged.
- Do not use an extension cord or power strip. Plug directly into a dedicated outdoor outlet.
- Do not run the cord under flooring, decking, or coverings where it can be pinched.
- Unplug before relocating, before cleaning or maintenance, and before any long shutdown.

## Minimum clearances

Every install must meet these distances, measured from the heater and rail to the nearest surface.

| Reference | Label | Minimum distance |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling, above rail | DIM A | 0 in (0 cm) |
| Wall, side of rail | DIM B | 20 in (51 cm) |
| Objects under the heater | DIM C | 36 in (91 cm) |
| Floor to bottom of rail | DIM D | 84 in (213 cm). Use 90 in (229 cm) where people walk under |
| Wall to the downward-facing side of the heater | DIM E | 20 in (51 cm) |

A heater node is 8 in in diameter on each side of the rail. Two rails mounted end to end need at least 4 in between them.

## Clearance rules

{% include dodont.html do="Mount above the table surface, out of the way of normal use." dont="Mount where people brush against the heater or its hot parts." %}

{% include dodont.html do="Keep awnings, umbrellas, and fabric at least 20 in from the heater." dont="Hang or drape anything on or near the rail or heaters." %}

- Never block or obstruct the front of the heater.
- Keep curtains, awnings, umbrellas, and fabric roofs out of direct radiant heat and at the specified clearances.
- Install only on solid surfaces capable of holding 300 lb.
- Keep the power cord away from hot surfaces and away from the heated air at the front of the heater.
- Do not power on until installation is complete, mounting is secure, and clearances are verified.

## When to stop and call Focal

Stop using the heater and contact Focal Support if you notice any of these:

- Physical damage to the housing or grille
- A burned, frayed, or damaged power cord or plug
- Unusual odors or sounds during operation
- The heater will not turn on or respond to controls
- Any other visible or suspected electrical or heating damage

The heater has no user-serviceable parts. All service beyond basic cleaning must be done by Focal-authorized personnel.
