## Logging in

Focal emails you a login link and a password. Open the link, enter the password, and you're in. There's no username, because the link is specific to your space.

Tap the menu icon next to the Focal logo to move between **Heater Control**, **Scheduling**, and **Settings**. Heater Control is where you run day to day.

## The Heater Control page

This is where you'll spend most of your time.

The **dashboard** at the top shows which mode your heaters are in, and in Schedule Mode, whether the schedule is active and what time heaters shut down. The **power button** turns every heater on or off at once. **System Status** shows how many heaters are online and active.

The **restaurant map** shows your zones. If you have multiple parklets or areas, Focal sets them up as separate zones. **All Heaters** and **zone controls** push one heat level to everything, or to everything in a zone, which is the fastest way to warm the space.

**Zone maps** below that show each heater in its real position on the rail, so units are easy to find. Tap a heater to open its own controls.

## How heat levels get set

Heat runs from OFF (0) to HIGH (3). During your scheduled hours, Focal sets the level for you based on the outdoor temperature and whether a guest is seated:

| Outdoor temperature | Heat level |
|---|---|
| Below 55°F | 3 (HIGH) |
| 55 to 60°F | 2 |
| 60 to 65°F | 1 |
| 65°F and above | No heat |

{% include diagram.html src="ctrl-03-heat-levels.svg" caption="Heat levels run from OFF (0) to HIGH (3)." %}

Changes aren't instant. Give it a few minutes to catch up after a guest sits down or the weather turns. When a guest leaves, the heater drops back to its unoccupied level after about five minutes.

Anyone can override the automatic level at any time, and a manual change always wins. Staff override from this page. Guests can scan the QR tag on their heater to open a simple page on their phone and adjust their own side, without flagging down staff. Outside your scheduled hours, neither works.

## Schedule Mode and Manual Mode

{% include dodont.html do="Keep Schedule Mode on. It defines when heat can run and shuts every heater off at the end of your hours." dont="Leave heaters in Manual Mode day to day. Nothing turns off on its own, so a unit can run all night if someone forgets." %}

**Schedule Mode** sets the hours heaters are allowed to run. At your start time nothing switches on by itself, heat simply becomes available: to staff, to guests scanning the QR tag, and to the automatic adjustment. At your end time every heater turns off. Outside your hours heaters can't be turned on at all, they show as disabled, their purple and orange lights are off, and they flash green if someone pulls the string.

**Manual Mode** hands full control to staff and guests. Heaters stay where they're set until someone changes them, and nothing shuts off at the end of the night. Use it when you need to run outside your normal hours: switch over, run the heaters, then switch back. Your saved hours aren't affected.

You pick the mode from Scheduling, or switch to Manual straight from the dashboard.

## Common tasks

**Skip an upcoming holiday.** Scheduling, uncheck the closed days, Save Settings.

**Warm the space before service.** On the Heater Control page, use All Heaters to push a level to everything at once. Works any time you're inside your scheduled hours.

**Run outside your hours once.** Switch to Manual Mode from the dashboard, run the heaters, then switch back to Schedule Mode.

**Change your zones or automatic levels.** Contact Focal and we'll set it up.

If a heater isn't behaving the way you expect, see [Troubleshooting]({{ site.baseurl }}/troubleshooting/).
